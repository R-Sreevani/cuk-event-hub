Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for events?

Browse events from the Events page, click on an event you're interested in, and click the "Register" button. Fill in your details and confirm registration.

Is there any registration fee?

Some events are free, while others may have a nominal fee. The fee is clearly mentioned on each event's details page.

How do I get my certificate?

Certificates are automatically generated after event completion. You can download them from your dashboard under "My Certificates".

Can I sync events to my calendar?

Yes! Use the Calendar Sync feature to add events to Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple Calendar with just one click.

How do I contact event organizers?

Each event page has contact information for the organizer. You can also use this contact form to reach the central team.

I forgot my password. What do I do?

Click on "Forgot Password" on the login page. Enter your email, and we'll send you instructions to reset your password.
