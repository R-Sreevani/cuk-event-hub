Our Mission

To provide a comprehensive, user-friendly platform that streamlines event organization, registration, and management for the Central University of Karnataka community. We aim to foster collaboration, enhance participation, and create memorable experiences for students, faculty, and staff.

Our Vision

To become the premier event management solution in academic institutions, setting the standard for digital transformation in campus event coordination. We envision a connected campus where every event reaches its full potential through technology.

Key Features

Easy Event Creation

Create and publish events in minutes with our intuitive interface. Add details, set capacities, and manage registrations seamlessly.

Smart Registration

Students can register for events with just a few clicks. Automatic confirmation emails and QR codes for check-in.

QR Check-in System

Efficient event check-in using QR codes. Scan and verify attendance in real-time with our mobile-friendly system.

Calendar Integration

Sync events with Google Calendar, Outlook, or Apple Calendar. Never miss an important event again.

Digital Certificates

Automatic certificate generation for attendees. Download and share verified certificates instantly.

Analytics Dashboard

Comprehensive insights for organizers. Track registrations, attendance, and engagement metrics.
